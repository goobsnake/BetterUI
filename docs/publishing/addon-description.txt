[SIZE="4"][B][COLOR="darkorange"]BetterUI - Gamepad Interface Overhaul[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]

BetterUI modernizes ESO's gamepad UI with cleaner navigation, richer tooltips, configurable layouts, and high-impact quality-of-life upgrades across Inventory, Banking (including Guild Bank), Vendors, the Guild Store, Companions, and your HUD.

[COLOR="darkorange"][SIZE="3"][B]Current Release Highlights (v3.07)[/B][/SIZE][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*][B]NEW[/B] Vendor & Fence module — buy, sell, buy back, repair, fence, launder, and train your mount through the full BetterUI interface
[*][B]NEW[/B] Guild Store module — browse with rich filters and saved search presets, sell with a real-time fee/profit preview, and manage your listings
[*][B]NEW[/B] Companion Equipment module — manage companion gear with search, sorting, batch actions, and Bind-on-Equip protection
[*][B]NEW[/B] Full Guild Bank support in Banking, including gold transfers and batch item moves
[*][B]NEW[/B] Gamepad screen-reader narration for BetterUI's list screens (Inventory, Banking, Vendor, Guild Store, Companions)
[*][B]NEW[/B] Movable HUD — drag orbs, bars, and frames anywhere, with per-element offset/scale controls and a position lock
[*][B]NEW[/B] Built-in diagnostics (/builog, /buihealth) with an optional privacy mode
[*]Faster list rebuilds, lower per-frame HUD overhead, and hardened compatibility with other addons
[/LIST]
